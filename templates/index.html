ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

Dr. T. Thimmaiah Institute of Technology

Established in 1986, Dr. T. Thimmaiah Institute of Technology (DR TTIT) is an engineering institution located at Oorgaum, Kolar Gold Fields, Karnataka - 563120.

Approval
